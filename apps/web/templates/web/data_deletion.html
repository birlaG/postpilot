You can request deletion of your PostPilot account and associated personal information by emailing {{ support_email }} from the email address associated with the account.

Include in your request

  • The email address used to sign in to PostPilot.
  • Your workspace name, if you know it.
  • The social platform account(s) you want disconnected, if you are requesting deletion of a specific connection only.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account before acting on a deletion request. Do not send your password, one-time passcode or full payment-card details.

What happens next

We will review your request and delete or de-identify information that we are not required to retain, subject to applicable legal, accounting, security and dispute-resolution obligations. Deleting a connection from PostPilot removes its stored authorization token; you can also revoke the app's access from the social platform's own settings. Deletion from PostPilot does not delete content already published to a third-party platform; remove that content directly on the platform.

For information about data use and retention, see our Privacy Policy.
